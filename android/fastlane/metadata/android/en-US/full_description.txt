Tolly is an unofficial, modern Android client for KU Leuven's Toledo, with your courses, announcements, agenda, grades and files in one app.

• Courses with their content, grades and recordings
• Which class is on now, and in which room
• Announcements with notifications
• Your timetable and Toledo's deadlines in one agenda
• Your KU Leuven mail in its own tab
• Downloads, and whole courses for offline use
• Two looks: Material 3 Expressive or Liquid Glass, light or dark
• Made for phones, tablets and foldables, in English and Dutch

You sign in on KU Leuven's own login page, so Tolly never sees your password. No ads, no tracking, no accounts.

AI was used in the development of the app. Tolly is open source: https://github.com/Bueezi/tolly
