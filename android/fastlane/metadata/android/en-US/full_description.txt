Tolly brings KU Leuven's Toledo to your phone and tablet: your courses, announcements, agenda, grades and files in one app that keeps working offline.

Tolly is an unofficial app. It is not made, endorsed or supported by KU Leuven.

COURSES
• This academic year's courses and communities, each with a subject icon and colour; rename them, pick another icon, drag them into your own order or flag them.
• See at a glance which course you have class in right now, until when and in which room, and when the next class starts.
• Folders, learning modules, pages, documents, files and links, as in Toledo. Tests and assignments open in a built-in browser without signing in again.
• Grades per course with feedback, and all your results together.
• Course recordings in a built-in player.

ANNOUNCEMENTS
• Per course and all together, with unread ones in bold and a filter for them.
• Optional notifications for new announcements, checked as often as you like.
• Swipe to mark as read or flag, as in a mail app.

AGENDA
• Your lessons from the KU Leuven timetable together with Toledo's deadlines and events, as a list, a day or a week.
• Deadlines on a screen of their own.

FILES AND OFFLINE
• Background downloads that resume, open in any app, share or save a copy, optionally automatically in a folder per course.
• Download a whole course for offline use, leaving out the big files you don't need.
• Everything you've seen stays available without a connection.

TWO LOOKS
• Material 3 Expressive with dynamic colour, or Liquid Glass with a floating glass tab bar.
• Light and dark, in English and Dutch, and built for tablets and foldables too, with resizable side-by-side panes.

PRIVACY
You sign in with KU Leuven's own login page, including two-factor authentication; Tolly never sees your password. Your data goes only between your device and KU Leuven's services: no ads, no tracking, no accounts of our own.

Tolly is open source: https://github.com/Bueezi/tolly
